Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2, 2026  ·  Last Updated: May 2, 2026

One Empire is operated by Shine Quek. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our content automation platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you:

  • Submit your weekly content brief through our intake form
  • Connect your social media accounts (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram)
  • Contact us for support

This information may include your name, email address, business details, content preferences, and OAuth access tokens for social platforms you choose to connect.

2. Social Media Integrations

When you authorise One Empire to connect to your social media accounts, we collect and store OAuth access tokens on your behalf. These tokens are used solely to publish content you have approved to your chosen platforms.

  • LinkedIn: We request w_member_social, openid, profile, and email scopes to post on your behalf.
  • Facebook & Instagram: We request pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement, pages_show_list, and instagram_content_publish to manage and publish page/profile content.

You may revoke access at any time through your social platform's account settings. Revoking access will prevent future automated posting but will not delete previously published content.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate AI-powered content based on your weekly brief
  • To schedule and publish approved content to connected social platforms
  • To deliver generated content to your email address
  • To maintain and improve our platform
  • To communicate with you about your account or our services

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. AI Content Generation

Content briefs you submit are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for AI-assisted content generation. Your brief data is processed by Anthropic in accordance with their Privacy Policy. We do not use your briefs to train AI models.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in Supabase (PostgreSQL). OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest. We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption for all data in transit.

Access to your data is restricted to authorised personnel and automated systems required to deliver our service.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the address below. Published social media posts are not deleted from platforms upon account closure — this must be done directly through the respective platform.

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate One Empire:

  • Anthropic Claude API — AI content generation
  • Supabase — Database and authentication
  • n8n (self-hosted) — Workflow automation
  • Netlify — Frontend hosting
  • Google Gmail API — Transactional email delivery
  • LinkedIn API — Social publishing
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) API — Social publishing

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Cookies

Our platform uses minimal session-based cookies necessary for authentication and OAuth state management. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
