# POL-202: Laptop and Hardware Policy

## Standard laptops

| Role | Standard device |
|---|---|
| Engineering | Dell Latitude 7440, 32 GB RAM |
| All other roles | Lenovo ThinkPad T14, 16 GB RAM |
| Sales (on request) | MacBook Air |

## Refresh cycle

- Laptops are replaced every **4 years** from the purchase date.
- An employee whose laptop is 4 or more years old is **eligible for a refresh**
  and can request one through the helpdesk (P4 ticket).
- Laptops younger than 4 years are replaced early only if they have a hardware
  fault that cannot be repaired.

## Hardware faults

- Hardware faults (battery swelling, broken screen, keyboard faults) are repaired
  under the manufacturer's warranty. Raise a P3 ticket.
- If you cannot work at all, the IT desk issues a **loaner laptop** the same day
  (P2 ticket).

## Lost or stolen devices

A lost or stolen laptop or phone with company data is a **security incident**.
Report it within **24 hours** - see POL-203. IT will remotely lock and wipe the
device.

## Personal devices

Personal laptops must not be used to access internal systems. Personal phones may
be used for email and Teams only through the Intune-managed Outlook and Teams apps.
