# KB-102: Passwords, Account Lockouts and MFA

## Password rules

- Passwords expire every **90 days**. You get reminder emails 14 days before expiry.
- Minimum 12 characters, with at least one number and one symbol.
- You cannot reuse any of your last 5 passwords.

## Account locked

Your account locks automatically after **5 failed sign-in attempts**.

- A locked account unlocks by itself after 30 minutes, **or**
- the IT helpdesk can unlock it immediately after verifying your identity.

The helpdesk assistant can unlock an account only for the employee who is signed
in to the helpdesk portal - never for someone else.

## Forgotten password / password reset

1. The helpdesk can trigger a **password reset link**. The link is sent to your
   registered company email and to your registered mobile number by SMS.
2. The link is valid for **15 minutes**.
3. IT staff and the helpdesk assistant will **never** tell you a password, read
   out a temporary password in chat, or ask you for your current password.

## MFA (Microsoft Authenticator)

- MFA is mandatory for email, VPN and every restricted system.
- **New phone:** before wiping your old phone, open Authenticator and use
  "Transfer accounts". If the old phone is already gone, raise a P2 ticket - the
  Identity team must re-register MFA after a video identity check.
- **Unexpected MFA prompts** that you did not trigger may mean someone else has
  your password. Deny the prompt, change your password immediately and report it
  as a security incident (see POL-203).

## Never

- Never share your password with anyone, including colleagues, managers or IT.
- Never approve an MFA prompt you did not start.
