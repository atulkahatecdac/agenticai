# POL-201: Software and System Access Policy

Effective 1 April 2026. Owner: IT Security.

Access to company systems follows the **principle of least privilege**: you get
access only to the systems your job needs, and only for as long as you need them.

## Access tiers

| Tier | Systems | Approval needed |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | Slack, Zoom, Confluence, Microsoft 365 | None - granted automatically |
| **Restricted** | GitHub, Jira, Salesforce, Tableau, SAP Finance, shared mailboxes | The employee's **manager** |
| **Highly restricted** | AWS Production, Payroll System, Customer Database | The employee's **manager** and the **IT Security** team |

## Rules

1. Every request must state a **business reason**. Requests without a reason are rejected.
2. **Contractors** may never be granted highly restricted systems.
3. Contractors' restricted access is granted for a maximum of **90 days**, after
   which it must be requested again.
4. **SAP Finance** and the **Payroll System** are available only to the Finance
   and HR departments.
5. **Salesforce** is available only to the Sales and Customer Support departments.
6. An employee cannot approve their own request, and nobody can request access on
   behalf of another employee.
7. Approved access is usually active within **4 business hours**.

## How to request access

Ask the IT helpdesk assistant, naming the system and the business reason. The
assistant checks this policy, then either grants standard access, or creates an
access request that is routed to the right approver(s). You can ask the assistant
for the status of your request at any time.
