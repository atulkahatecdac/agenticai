Returns policy

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
The item must be unused and in its original packaging.
Gift cards, socks and underwear cannot be returned for hygiene and security reasons.
Sale items marked "final sale" cannot be returned.
To start a return, the customer needs the order ID (format ORD- followed by 8 digits).
Return shipping is free for orders above 2,000 rupees; otherwise a 99 rupee fee is taken from the refund.
